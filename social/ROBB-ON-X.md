## Formats that feel human

### 1. Reply (highest trust)
Under a demo / funding post / factory video:

> Operator-in-the-loop is fine. Sell it as a service with a person in the stack, not as “full autonomy next quarter.”

> Cool gait. Show me tote-to-tote for a full shift when the pallet wrap is torn.

Rules: reply to **substance**. Don’t reply “🔥” or drop your link under strangers.

### 2. Quote-tweet
Original post stays visible; Robb adds the frame:

> $2.5B SPAC is a scoreboard, not a product. After close I care about redemptions, whether Digit v5 hits the order stack, and if “cooperative safety” is certifiable or slideware.

Optional second reply with blog link if you wrote the piece.

### 3. Standalone take (no link)
> SF now has competing humanoid clean prices you can argue about over coffee. That’s a healthier market signal than another “home robot someday” deck.

### 4. Soft blog pointer
> Wrote this up because the chart is fun and incomplete: robots-in-building ≠ robots-at-customers earning hours.  
> [link]

Or even better: post the take **without** link; if people ask, drop the article in a reply.

### 5. Pattern / trend
> Pattern this month: capital is still loud, but the interesting posts have a **price**, a **city**, or **hours on a floor**. Everything else is atmosphere.

## Hard rules (no exceptions)

1. **No fabricated takes.** If it isn’t in a source you saw or an article you wrote from sources, don’t post it as fact.
2. **Hedge company charts** (“company figure,” “per their release”).
3. **Don’t dunk for sport** on named engineers. Critique the claim, not the person.
4. **Don’t thread-dump the whole article** into 12 tweets.
5. **Don’t auto-like/follow/engage-pod.** Organic only.
6. **Disclosure:** if a post is basically promoting your article, that’s fine; don’t pretend it’s neutral news wire.
7. **API auto-post of this persona = optional later.** Default is human (or agent-drafted → human paste). Auto-reply bots will get the account killed and feel fake.

## Agent workflow (when user asks “be Robb on X”)

```
1. Scan list (24–48h) for leads — notes only
2. Cross-check anything you’ll state as fact (company post, press, or our article Sources)
3. Draft 5–15 items mixed: replies, QTs, standalones (see queue file format)
4. Mark each draft: REPLY | QT | SOLO | SOFT-LINK
5. For REPLY/QT: include target post URL or @handle + post id if known
6. User posts what they like — agent does not claim to have posted unless API is explicitly wired later
```

Output file: append to `social/robb-organic-queue.md` (or a dated section).
