## 6 generic reply lines (use when the target fits)

1. **Polished autonomy demo:**  
   `Looks clean on camera. Supervised, scripted, or unsupervised for a full shift? That answer changes the story.`

2. **Mega-round, thin product:**  
   `Runway isn’t product. First paid deployment + cost per hour of useful work — that’s the slide I want.`

3. **“Replace all labor” rhetoric:**  
   `Start with one cell, one SOP, one safety case. Replacement talk is cheap; takt time isn’t.`

4. **Home robot vapor:**  
   `Price, city, what’s off-limits, human still in the loop or not. Without those it’s a concept trailer.`

5. **Shipment flex without uptime:**  
   `Units shipped ≠ units still earning their keep six months later. Field support is the product.`

6. **Only dance/backflip clip:**  
   `Athletics ≠ work. I’ll rewatch the clip. I’ll believe the platform when it clears a boring shift metric.`
