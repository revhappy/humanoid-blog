China opened the **2026 World Robot Conference** in Beijing on Wednesday. **Associated Press** reporter E. Eduardo Castillo walked the floor at the Beiren Yichuang International Convention and Exhibition Centre and found boxing, dancing, ping pong, and a helper robot that could not fold a shirt.

Organizers say around **3,000 products** will be on show over five days. AP noted there was no sign of other countries exhibiting.

## What the floor actually showed

The Unitree booth was a main draw. AP says some of its models boxed and danced. One played ping pong with a human opponent. The conference opened the same day as **Unitree’s Shanghai stock debut**.

**UBTECH** brought industrial models that moved components the way they might on a factory floor. The crowd, AP reports, went harder for humanoids with lifelike faces sold as emotional-care machines.

> Jiao Jichao, deputy president of UBTECH Robotics, said one of its emotional-care humanoids can talk, make various facial expressions, rotate its neck and move its hands. And it is available to individual buyers. The price: 168,000 yuan (more than $24,000).

That is a real sticker. It is also a companion pitch, not a shift-ready worker.

## The demo that didn’t finish

AP is blunt about the gap. Several companies showed helper robots with mechanical arms and hands. At one booth, a robot spent several minutes trying to fold a shirt and could not do it.

The piece also notes the U.S. Federal Communications Commission recently banned imports of new foreign-made humanoid robots and quadrupeds, citing national security. That is the backdrop, not the floor show.

## A Human's Take

I am here for the ping-pong rally more than the IPO ticker. A humanoid that can box in a ring and still lose to a T-shirt is the honest 2026 snapshot.

The UBTECH face at **168,000 yuan** is a product, not a promise. If someone actually buys it for care work, I want hours, not expressions. Until then, the conference is doing what these shows do best: packing the hall, then reminding you the laundry is still on you.
