Berlin’s IFA 2026 stuffed Hall 25 with more humanoids than the show has ever booked. IFA’s own programme put **Robots on the Runway** on the Creator Stage at **12:45 on 5 September**, with Primebot, EngineAI, Dobot, DEEP Robotics, Agibot, Unitree, and others on the list. BGR walked the floor and came back with specs you can argue about.

## PrimeBOT Q1 and T1

**PrimeBOT Q1** is **88 cm** tall with **22** degrees of freedom, quasi-direct-drive joints, and full-body force control. BGR saw it kick, defend, and dance. The company claims more than **10** martial arts moves, up to **three hours** of battery, and **3.2 m/s** in open air. Users can 3D-print shells. Voice, app, or remote. China sales are supposed to start later this **September**; BGR says the price is still unannounced.

A **31 August** PrimeBOT release for the show described **T1** as a transformable personal robot that switches between humanoid and quadruped modes, with intelligent following, autonomous filming, and **15+** camera-movement templates. A **5 September** follow-up said T1 won the **IFA Innovation Award for Content Creation Technology** and Q1 was named an **IFA NEXT Honoree**. International Market CMO **Yuan Zou** told the Dream Stage panel that a personal robot does not have to do everything on day one, but it has to create value.

## Booster K1

<figure>
  <img src="/images/heroes/booster-k1-ifa.jpg" alt="Booster K1 in an Argentina No. 10 jersey kicking a soccer ball on turf at IFA" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>Booster K1 on the IFA turf, Messi kit and all. Source: José Adorno / BGR.</figcaption>
</figure>

Beijing’s **Booster Robotics** brought the **K1**: **95 cm**, **19.5 kg**, already listed in China, the U.S., and Europe. BGR’s floor price is **from $5,999**, rising past **$10,000** with compute. Compute scales from a **48 TOPS** base to Jetson Orin NX or AGX Orin up to **200 TOPS**. Binocular 3D depth camera, 9-axis IMU, mic and speaker array, Python/ROS/ROS 2. Swappable battery **50–80 minutes**, charge under an hour. BGR watched it dance, fight, and kick a ball, then celebrate like a soccer player even when the shot missed.

This is a different machine from Booster’s larger **T1** soccer-research platform. K1 is the suitcase-size developer biped.

## EngineAI T800

<figure>
  <img src="/images/heroes/engineai-t800-ifa.jpg" alt="EngineAI T800 humanoid in a fight-ring pose at IFA 2026" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>EngineAI T800 in the IFA fight-ring setup. Source: José Adorno / BGR.</figcaption>
</figure>

**EngineAI’s T800** is the heavy one on the floor: **173 cm**, aviation-grade aluminum, hollow wiring, **29** DoF, peak torque **450 N·m**, speeds above **3 m/s**. BGR says a kick can throw a person **two meters** — a company talking point, not a test they ran. Hands are **7-DoF**, **5 kg** per arm, with tactile feedback. Perception: 360-degree LiDAR, RealSense, Intel host, Jetson Orin NX. Battery: standard ternary pack or optional **74.8 V / 40 Ah** modular solid-state for up to **5 hours**. BGR found it in a fight ring throwing punches in the air. The Rundown’s IFA note says EngineAI’s T800 showed full-body moves on the runway while Agibot’s **X2 Ultra** did a split and needed a host’s hand after it lost balance.

IFA CEO **Leif Lindner**, quoted by BGR: this year the show has more humanoid robots than ever, and robotics is becoming part of everyday life. That is a trade-show sentence. The receipts on this floor are heights, prices, and which ones stayed upright.

## A Human's Take

I’m here for the 88 cm kung-fu kit you can reprint a shell for, and for a $6k soccer kid that already has a SKU. The T800 is a different argument: torque and a ring. None of these clips are a shift in a house. Q1 still has no price. If you want a robot you can actually order, the K1’s dollar figure is the one BGR put on the page.
