**DexForce** used the World Robot Conference to show wheeled humanoids packing mobile phones into boxes. Reuters, via *The Indian Express* on 20 August, says those machines have been on a **Lens Technology** line since early this year. Lens makes touchscreens and other parts for Apple and Huawei.

DexForce official **Nicole Yang** told Reuters the robots hold millimetre-level accuracy and can catch a phone sitting at a wrong angle.

> “In theory, human beings are the most dexterous,” Yang said. “But many workers in factories are unwilling to do this kind of boring work.”

That is a deployment claim with a named factory, not a booth-only packing demo.

## What the W1 Pro is

DexForce’s English product page calls **W1 Pro** an “AI-defined” wheeled humanoid. Stated specs:

- **1636 mm** tall, **110 kg**
- **34 DoF** on the differential chassis, **40 DoF** if you take the omni option
- Dual **7-DoF** arms, **10 kg** single-arm payload, arm repeatability **±0.5 mm** (without the hand)
- Dexterous hand at **6** active DoF per hand, or a 2-finger gripper
- **1000 Hz** whole-body control, **≥300 TOPS** on a dual-PC setup
- Battery **≥8 hours**, **1.5 m/s** max straight-line, rated **1.2 m/s**
- 6 cm baseline binocular head, optional wrist RGB-D, two lidars for SLAM

A stationary **W1 Core** drops the chassis and lists **22 DoF**.

The company is a 3D-vision shop that grew into a full robot. The product page’s manufacturing stills are factory floors and conveyors, which matches the Lens story better than a dance clip.

Reuters does not give a unit count at Lens, a cycle time, or an uptime figure. Millimetre-level is Yang’s phrasing; the spec sheet’s arm repeatability is ±0.5 mm without the hand, so treat the packing accuracy as a company claim until Lens publishes its own.

## A Human's Take

A named contract manufacturer boxing phones since early 2026 is the kind of sentence I want more of. I still want the boring numbers: how many robots, how many hours, how often a human still has to straighten the phone. Until then it is a real customer with a thin public scoreboard.
