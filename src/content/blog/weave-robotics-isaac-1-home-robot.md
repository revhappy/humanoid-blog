**Weave Robotics** put a price tag on the home-robot daydream: **Isaac 1** is available for preorder at **$7,999** upfront or **$449 a month**, in five pastel colors, with first **California** shipments targeted for **fall 2026**.

That’s from **Business Insider**’s launch and CEO interview coverage (July 2 and July 9, 2026). The San Francisco startup is **Y Combinator**-backed, founded by former **Apple** engineers **Kaan Dogrusoz** and **Evan Wineland**, and is leaning hard into **laundry** as the first real job.

## Price, Form, and Ambition

- **Wheeled** base — Dogrusoz tells BI wheels keep the robot passively balanced so a power loss doesn’t mean a fall
- Fabric exterior (“Weave” is the brand and the casing idea)
- Tasks claimed: laundry, bed making, tidy-up; website says autonomous by default with remote operator backup when stuck
- Goal: **1,000 U.S. homes by end of 2027**
- Today: a small number of earlier **Isaac 0** units in California homes; company has **20** employees; more robots deployed than employees, Dogrusoz said without giving exact counts

At **$7,999**, BI notes Isaac undercuts **1X NEO**’s roughly **$20,000** expected price. Launch chatter on X drew millions of views — and the usual “slow” comments. Dogrusoz’s answer: quality first; T-shirt folds that once took up to four minutes are closer to **two** now; the product promise is work done while you’re at work, not a race against a human for the camera.

## Autonomy With a Human in the Loop

Dogrusoz is explicit with BI: Isaac 0 units run **mostly** autonomously, but **teleoperation interventions** still happen for unseen conditions. Customers know that. New features may need remote help first; the autonomy boundary expands task by task, starting with folding.

That puts Weave in the same fall-2026 home window as **Sunday’s Memo** beta and **1X** deliveries — different price points, different honesty levels about remote assist.

## A Human's Take

Ship fall, publish the intervention rate — then we can talk about “never folding laundry again.”
