**CasiaHand Robotics**, incubated at the CAS Institute of Automation, used WRC 2026 for a fourth consecutive appearance and a simple flex: leave a dexterous hand running. Gasgoo says the **M6** cell on the floor was livestreaming continuous picks of milk cartons, medicine boxes, and cosmetics, holding around **30°C**, at more than **1,000 grasps per hour**.

The company calls it the first 24-hour livestream of an industry-grade dexterous hand. I cannot independently confirm the “world’s first” part. The booth photos match a grasping cell with a monitor counting cycles.

## Six hands, three series

Gasgoo lists a six-product debut across **M**, **X**, and three-finger industrial **G**:

- **M6**: anthropomorphic, lightweight, **6 DoF**, multimodal sensing, **10 kg** payload
- **M11**: worm gears plus cable drive, hybrid rigid-soft
- **X12**: full direct-drive, **12 DoF**, **30 kg** payload, industrial brief

The **Brain-Si 0.5** stack is described as a large brain for scene understanding and a small brain for joints. The grasping platform, Gasgoo says, does not need a pre-set object pose. A separate **Wuji** industrial robot opened machine doors and swapped trays without a factory rebuild. Visitors could try a data glove mapping onto a hand.

## Sensors on order

A week before the show, Gasgoo reported a deal with **Fulai New Materials** (605488): CasiaHand will buy **200,000** tactile-sensor sets to support **40,000** industry-grade hands. Capacity targets are nearly **30,000** units a year by the end of 2026 and more than **100,000** by late 2027. Cumulative H1 2026 orders, the company said, already topped **100 million yuan**. Core parts are claimed 100% domestic. CEO Zhang Tianyi told Gasgoo the fight has moved from hardware-only to hardware plus the model.

Those production numbers are CasiaHand’s, via one outlet. Treat them as targets until shipments show up.

## A Human's Take

A thousand grasps an hour on mixed packaging, live, is a better demo than a polished six-second clip. Temperature staying near 30°C is the unglamorous detail that tells you they are thinking about a shift. Forty thousand hands is a slide until the sensors land and the yield holds.
