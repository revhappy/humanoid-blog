**Aimoga Robotics**, the robot brand incubated by Chinese automaker **Chery**, has passed **2,000 cumulative overseas deliveries**, with products and services now covering **more than 60 countries and regions**, according to **CnEVPost** reporting on a **July 30, 2026** ceremony in Wuhu, Anhui.

## From 300 humanoids to a wider export push

CnEVPost notes the overseas tally is a sharp step up from year-end **2025**, when Aimoga had delivered **300 humanoid robots** and **1,000 robot dogs** to more than **30** countries and regions.

The expansion path, as reported:

- Overseas start in **Malaysia** (2025), with robots used in **automotive marketing and service**
- Later moves into Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe, Africa, and Latin America
- Showcase at **Automation Thailand 2026** (March)
- First AI experience center in **Vietnam** open in **July**

Chery held a send-off in Wuhu where trucks left the **Chery International Industrial Park** for **Wuhu Port**. Aimoga says it holds **EU CE** and **U.S. FCC** certifications.

## Where the robots work — and what they cost

Reported use cases skew commercial and public, not pure factory biped theater:

- Automotive marketing and services  
- Public-space guidance  
- Smart policing and medical guidance  
- Commercial reception  

In China, **110** Aimoga police robots were deployed across several cities in the first half of **2026** for traffic control and public-order guidance, per the same report.

Consumer pricing on **JD.com** (flagship store opened April):

- Humanoid: **¥285,800** (~**$42,100** at the article’s exchange rate)  
- Quadruped robot dog: **¥15,800**

Aimoga also signed agreements with **more than 300 dealers** at a recruitment event earlier this year. Chery founded Aimoga in **January 2025** and frames it as a third growth engine alongside the car business.

**Zhang Guibing**, Chery executive vice president and Aimoga general manager, stressed that overseas competition needs more than product specs — R&D, manufacturing, supply chain, distribution, delivery, service, and local operations.
