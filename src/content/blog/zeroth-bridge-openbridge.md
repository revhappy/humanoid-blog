**Zeroth** started global bookings for **Bridge** on **2 September 2026**: an 88-centimeter, about 13-kilogram humanoid aimed at developers, plus **OpenBridge**, an open-source stack the company wants treated like a shared workshop rather than a locked product.

The Suzhou firm, founded in 2024, is selling a first batch of the **Bridge Geek Edition**. ACCESS Newswire carried the launch. Interesting Engineering matched the size, two-finger grippers, and 30–40 cm arms.

## What ships

ACCESS Newswire and the company site describe Bridge as a compact, desktop-friendly body with proprietary high-torque actuator modules. Out of the box the press kit lists:

- **Bipedal walking** and obstacle avoidance
- A library of human-like motions and choreography
- **SDKs**, open motion-control APIs, and motion-capture / VR hooks

Interesting Engineering quotes the YouTube description claiming dynamic locomotion, backflips, and full-body teleoperation. That is the company’s own copy, not an independent lab test.

OpenBridge is the software layer. The site splits it into a **Robot Hub** (hardware specs, drivers, SDKs), a **Skill Hub** for reusable skills, and a **Simulation Hub**. GitHub orgs for those hubs are linked from [zeroth-openbridge.com](https://zeroth-openbridge.com/).

## Price, with a hedge

The official shop lists **founding access at CNY 5,999**. ACCESS Newswire says Bridge is available for global booking from 2 September through that site, and that **price may vary by region**. I am not treating any third-party dollar conversion as a published MSRP.

Zeroth is also running a **Bridge Builder** program. First phase: **500** developers shortlisted. **200** get complimentary access to Bridge and OpenBridge co-creation. The other **300** get priority Geek Edition purchase, SDK access, and a later shot at the program.

## A Human's Take

An 88 cm, 13 kg biped you can actually book is the right shape for a developer kit. I care less about the “Android of robots” line than whether Robot Hub and Skill Hub stay public once the first Geek units leave Suzhou. CNY 5,999 founding access is cheap enough to try; the regional-price hedge on the press wire is the part I’ll watch when a cart total appears.
