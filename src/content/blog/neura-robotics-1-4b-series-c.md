**NEURA Robotics**, the Metzingen-based builder of the **4NE1** humanoid, closed a **Series C of up to $1.4 billion** — described by the company (via **Trending Topics**) as the largest capital injection to date for a full-stack robotics firm. Investors named in that report include **Tether**, **Qualcomm Technologies**, **Amazon**, **NVIDIA**, **imec.xpand**, **Bosch**, **Schaeffler**, the **European Investment Bank**, **Lingotto Horizon**, and **InterAlpen Partners**.

## What the money is for

According to Trending Topics’ company-sourced write-up, proceeds go to:

- Global deployment of cognitive and humanoid robots
- Expanding the **Neuraverse** platform (shared skill/learning network)
- Rolling out **NEURA Gyms** — real-world training environments
- Building production and scaling capacity toward a stated goal of **several million robots by 2030**

NEURA also claimed an **order backlog and deployment pipeline exceeding $1 billion**. Treat backlog claims as company-reported unless independent order books surface.

CEO and founder **David Reger** put the thesis in plain language: AI that “will not simply take place on screens” but moves and works in the physical world. The company positions **Neuraverse** as an open ecosystem where robots exchange skills and learning, rather than isolated fleets.

## The 4NE1 hardware brief

NEURA’s product page for **4NE1** lists:

- Height **~180 cm**, weight **~80 kg**, speed up to **5 km/h**
- Payload range **10–100 kg** (task/config dependent)
- Full-body sensing, multimodal AI, reinforcement learning
- Industrial and home variants; wheeled options; **4NE1 Mini** reserved for **Spring 2026** research/education use cases

That is Europe’s attempt at a full-stack humanoid company with serious industrial partners on the cap table — Bosch and Schaeffler are not tourist investors.

## A Human's Take

$1.4B is enough capital to force a binary: either Europe gets a real humanoid OEM with factories and field data, or this becomes a very expensive science project. I care about **NEURA Gym data volume**, **unit cost curves**, and whether that **$1B+ pipeline** turns into multi-year revenue. Investor logos are impressive. Shift logs will be decisive.
