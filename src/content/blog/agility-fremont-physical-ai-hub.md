**Agility Robotics** opened a **60,000-square-foot** facility in **Fremont, California** (announced July 16, 2026) as a **Physical AI** hub for teaching **Digit** new skills. Manufacturing stays at **RoboFab** in Salem, Oregon. Software, skill training, and deployment support get a Bay Area address — minutes from where Tesla is expected to scale **Optimus**.

Industry story, still fun: the company already moving totes for real customers just bought itself a bigger classroom.

## What Agility Says the Site Does

From the company press release:

- Software and capabilities hub for AI that lets Digit learn more sophisticated warehouse and factory work
- Plans to hire nearly **200** people across AI/ML engineering and field ops
- Complements Salem manufacturing rather than replacing it
- Timed ahead of Agility’s planned public listing via **Churchill Capital Corp XI**

CEO **Peggy Johnson**: being in Silicon Valley’s talent pool should speed new Digit capabilities into customer sites.

## Commercial Context

Agility’s release lists active deployments with **Schaeffler**, **GXO**, **Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada**, and **Mercado Libre**. It claims more than **$300 million** of multi-year orders for **Digit v5**, subject to contractual milestones, plus a pipeline of **over 30 customers**.

TechCrunch’s coverage adds color: Digit already generates revenue hauling totes; Digits have moved **100,000+ totes** at a GXO site (per prior Agility numbers); Digit v5, expected this fall, is meant to sense humans and work outside pure robot-only zones. Co-founder **Damion Shelton** stressed that safety stacks should **not** be generative AI — creativity belongs in applications, not brakes.

## A Human's Take

Fremont is a flex and a necessity. If you are the rare humanoid shop with actual customer throughput, you need a place to train skills as fast as the pipeline grows. I care less about the Optimus neighborhood jokes and more about whether those 200 hires turn multi-year order sheets into robots that still hit safety bars when the tote pile is messy.
