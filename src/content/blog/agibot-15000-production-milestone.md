**AGIBOT** (Zhiyuan Robotics) says its **15,000th robot** rolled off the production line in **June 2026**. The milestone unit is an **AGIBOT G2**, an industrial-grade wheeled embodied robot built for factory and real-world task work, according to the company’s **June 28** announcement.

## The ramp, in the company’s numbers

AGIBOT’s own timeline:

- Founded **February 2023**
- Roughly **one year** from first **1,000** units to **5,000**
- **5,000 → 10,000** in about **three months**, with production speed described as more than **4×** the prior phase
- **15,000th** unit celebrated **June 28, 2026**

A company graphic on the same announcement breaks cumulative roll-off by line: **A Series 3,547**, **X Series 6,726**, **G Series 4,763** — totaling the **15,000** claim.

## Why they say scale is the point

**Dr. Yao Maoqing**, partner and president of AGIBOT’s Embodied AI Business Unit, framed the number as both a manufacturing milestone and a signal that the industry is moving from proof-of-concept toward scaled deployment.

The company also points to field validation: roughly **100 cumulative hours** of factory livestream operations with **G2** on a tablet mass-production quality-inspection section, running alongside line workers and factory cadence.

On market share, AGIBOT cites **Omdia** research ranking it first globally in humanoid robot **shipments** for **2025** — **5,168 units** and about **39%** share — a figure the company uses to connect last year’s shipping leadership to this year’s production tally. (Shipment rankings and production roll-off are related but not identical metrics.)

**The Robot Report** covered the same milestone, describing G2 as a wheeled mobile manipulator with a humanoid torso and arms for industrial tasks.
