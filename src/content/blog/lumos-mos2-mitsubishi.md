**Lumos Robotics** is selling **MOS 2** as a factory worker, not a stage act: a **1,650 mm** wheeled dual-arm on an omnidirectional base, **30 kg** rated / **50 kg** max across both arms, already doing inspection, box pushing, screwdriving, and material handling on **Mitsubishi Electric** PLC lines.

The product page is live at [lumosbot.tech](https://www.lumosbot.tech/products/mos). Embodied Global’s **28 August 2026** write-up, based on a Lumos press release, is the secondary I used for the Mitsubishi deployment and the CEO quotes.

## The machine, not the round

Specs from the MOS 2 page:

- **Height** 1,650 mm; mass **≤350 kg** including battery; base **735 × 700 × 300 mm**
- **22** degrees of freedom; max joint torque **380 N·m**; seven-DoF arms with a standard flange
- Travel **≤1.5 m/s**; lift travel **750 mm**; trench **≤35 mm**; step **≤25 mm**; gradeability **≤5%**
- Dual **19.5 Ah** LiFePO4 packs, hot-swappable, runtime **≥8 h**; **NVIDIA Orin AGX, 275 TOPS**
- Standard sensing: two base 3D lidars, two wrist cameras, one head camera, bumper strip, two 6-axis force-torque sensors
- Optional chest camera and a rear-top lidar; VR teleop for hazardous work

Embodied Global puts the footprint at about **0.5 m²**, small enough for narrow aisles. That matches the “compact” pitch on the product page.

First-generation **MOS** units, not MOS 2, logged **more than 1,000 hours** of continuous, failure-free operation at Mitsubishi’s **Changshu** plant, according to that same report. The new machine is the one Lumos is putting on core PLC lines for six-sided visual inspection plus handling.

> "Dancing, running and doing backflips may demonstrate a robot's physical capabilities, but they do not prove its industrial value," said **Yu Chao**, founder and CEO, in the press material Embodied Global quotes. "What industrial customers ultimately care about is task success rate, production cycle time, deployment time and return on investment."

Mitsubishi Electric opened four scenarios to Lumos in 2025 (inspection, assembly, sorting, handling) and later led A1/A2 rounds. I am not making this a funding story. The useful fact is customer-then-investor on a live PLC line.

## A Human's Take

Wheels, 50 kg, eight-hour packs, and a named automaker line is the adult version of “humanoid in a factory.” I want cycle time and how often a person still has to swap the end effector. Until Lumos publishes those, treat 1,000 hours as a gen-1 endurance claim, not a MOS 2 scorecard.
