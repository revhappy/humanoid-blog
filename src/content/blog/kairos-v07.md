South Korea's state humanoid is a year old and still needs a lift onto its feet. That is not a dunk. It is what **KIMM** put on a Seoul stage.

On **September 7, 2026**, the Korea Institute of Machinery & Materials showed **KAIROS V0.7** at the 2026 Global Machinery Technology Forum, marking the institute's **50th** anniversary. Aju Press and Maeil Business both covered the demo. Four of six units performed. Each had to be lowered from hangers and worked into a starting pose.

## A slow dance, on purpose

KAIROS stands about **170 cm**, weighs roughly **80 kg**, and has **29 degrees of freedom**, according to the institute. V0.5, shown in April, walked, shook hands, and waved. V0.7 adds whole-body motion: national calisthenics and **talchum**, Korea's masked dance, with hansam sleeves and a yangban mask.

Aju Press watched the joints. Arms rose to shoulder height and stopped. Commands took a beat. Feet stayed almost glued to the floor. Park Dong-il, director of KIMM's Advanced Robotics Research Center, said the institute started humanoid research a little over a year ago, from work launched in **May 2025**.

A glass-of-water handoff was planned and dropped. "Technical difficulties barred us from the skit today," Park said, in Aju Press's account.

The six machines are not clones. Park said each carries a different mix of reducers and motors to test different roles.

## A schedule, and a budget ask

**V1** is dated **April 2027**. **V2** is **2030**. Park Chan-hoon, who leads KIMM's AI Robotics Institute and the "Autonomous Growth AI Humanoid" group, told the hall that speed is the race and asked for at least **3 billion won** (about **$2.2 million**) budgeted. Aju Press quoted him: "We will answer it with results."

Maeil Business says KIMM wants the platform for manufacturing, disaster response, defense, and later household work. Seoul National University's Zhang Byoung-tak framed humanoid AI as the wave after generative and agentic models. Doosan Robotics CEO Kim Min-pyo also spoke.

This is a national-lab platform, not a product. The researchers bowed with the robots and asked lawmakers to keep funding it.

## A Human's Take

I am so here for a lab that will put a wobbly dance on a government stage instead of cutting a kung-fu reel. The hanger is the story. If V1 in April actually stands up without a crane, Korea has a body it can argue about. Until then, this is a calendar and a costume.
