LimX Dynamics put **five LimX Luna** humanoids on the 2026 **CHINA GT** opening-ceremony grid and ran a group dance. The company posted the clip on YouTube on **17 September**. Robostart covered it the next day.

The pitch is not a new robot. It is five of them, on a racetrack, moving together.

## What LimX says the clip shows

The [YouTube description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I0lJ3zv8mPc) credits **stable whole-body motion control** and **intelligent multi-robot coordination**. [Robostart](https://robotstart.info/article/2026/09/18/382434.html) repeats that line and notes the robots keep large arm swings and direction changes lined up. LimX frames the show as a step into motorsports, live entertainment, and commercial events.

“Autonomous” here is the company’s word for the group number. The posted video is a performance, not a timed trial with a public miss count.

## The body behind the routine

Luna launched in May as LimX’s full-size interactive humanoid. The [English newsroom note](https://www.limxdynamics.com/en/news/BK000062) lists:

- **160 cm** tall
- **27** body degrees of freedom, not counting end effectors
- **System 0** whole-body motion model
- China retail **RMB 298,000**; first 100 units **RMB 40,000** off
- Video-to-motion learning, manual teaching, and **10** shipped dance routines in LimX Studio
- Cluster sync sold as millisecond-class for up to **200** units
- Up to **4 hours** per charge (LimX calls that a **150%** generational jump) plus external power for 24/7
- Core cooling metrics down **30%** versus the prior generation, per the same note

The CHINA GT clip is five units, not 200. The launch card is still the spec sheet the dance is sitting on.

## A Human's Take

Five identical bodies keeping time on a live grid is a harder party trick than one robot hitting a split in a white cyclorama. I still want the boring extras: how they recover when one unit drifts, who is calling the clock, and whether a mall operator can restage this from a phone video the way the Studio app promises. Until then, I’ll take the racetrack clip as a coordination demo, not a labor one.
