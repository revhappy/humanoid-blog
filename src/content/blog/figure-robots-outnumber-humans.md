Sometimes the graph is the story. In June 2026, **Figure AI** CEO **Brett Adcock** posted that **for the first time, robots outnumber humans at Figure**.

India Today’s write-up of that announcement (citing Adcock’s chart) puts approximate shapes on the curves:

- Robot count rising sharply after manufacturing started, **crossing ~100 by end of 2025** and reaching roughly **740 by Q2 2026**
- Human headcount climbing from near zero in 2022 to about **400 by end of 2025**, then toward **~650–660 in 2026**

Those numbers are company-presented via the CEO’s public chart, filtered through secondary reporting — not an audited headcount filing. Treat them as Figure’s own scoreboard.

## Why People Care

Figure has been among the loudest US humanoid stories: BMW work, BotQ production ambitions, huge private valuation chatter. “More robots than employees” is a sticky line. It signals that manufacturing is real enough to stack metal on the floor — even if many of those robots are still training, testing, or staging rather than earning customer revenue.

The company has also talked publicly about ramping **Figure 03** production and factory deployment plans; this milestone is about **internal fleet vs staff**, not “robots replaced the workforce.”
