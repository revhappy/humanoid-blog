**UBTECH** is pushing humanoids out of the factory aisle and into a consumer brand. At its **2026 Global Launch Event** in Shenzhen (**June 30, 2026**), the company unveiled the **UWORLD U1 Series** — full-size “ultra-bionic” humanoids it calls the first designed for **mass production** in that category. Pricing starts at **¥119,800**, and UBTECH’s PR Newswire release says cumulative orders had already topped **13,361 units** as of launch day.

## Three models, one consumer push

The U1 lineup:

- **U1 Lite** — semi-torso edition
- **U1 Pro** — high-performance full-body
- **U1 Ultra** — high-dynamic full-body

Specs and claims from the official release:

- **88 degrees of freedom**
- Dual-pivot biomimetic cervical spine; claim of replicating up to **~90%** of fundamental human movements
- Emotion-aware LLM aimed at long-term companionship; **20+** fine-grained emotional states, **>90%** accuracy claim
- Speech-to-lip sync latency claim: within **20 ms**
- “Fast-and-slow brain” architecture with **~500 ms** intuitive responses plus large-model deep reasoning
- Privacy stack: local-first, minimal cloud, user-controlled hardware safeguards

CEO and founder **James Zhou** cast 2026 as the bridge from industrial/commercial Walker products into **consumer** adoption. Brand chief **Michael Tam** framed companionship as a mental-well-being product category and cited internal market projections for China’s ultra-bionic segment over the next decade (tens of billions of RMB scaling toward trillion-RMB language — treat as company outlook, not verified market fact).

## Companionship initiative + industrial second engine

UWORLD announced a **Human-Robot Companionship Initiative**, with plans to donate **100 customized U1 units in 2026** for vulnerable groups — including children growing up apart from parents and older adults living alone. Custom units may use **3D facial reconstruction** and **voiceprint** replication of designated individuals, per the release.

On the industrial side, UBTECH says the **Walker S Series** is already in mass production and delivery. UWORLD is positioned as a **second growth engine** after the company claimed a **2025** global lead in full-sized embodied intelligent humanoid revenue and sales volume (company claim).
