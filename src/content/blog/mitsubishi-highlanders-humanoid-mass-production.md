**Mitsubishi Motors** is not waiting for someone else to figure out factory humanoids. With University of Tokyo spinout **Highlanders**, the automaker has set a public target: **1,000 AI-equipped bipedal robots a month by the end of 2027**, while putting units on a Kyoto engine line to collect the data that actually matters.

That number comes from Kyodo reporting carried by **The Mainichi** and **Kyodo News** English — not a vague “scale ambition.” The companies also signed a formal MOU on **July 9, 2026**, covering joint development for Mitsubishi plants and mass production of Highlanders products at unused buildings on the Kyoto plant.

## What the Deal Actually Says

Mitsubishi’s own newsroom is careful: this is an **MOU** to *explore* joint development and Kyoto production, with feasibility work aimed at starting production in **early 2027**. Highlanders, founded in **May 2023**, builds general-purpose humanoids and quadrupeds with domestically sourced parts and already runs a factory in Tokorozawa, Saitama.

Parallel tracks, if they hold:

- **Use robots on Mitsubishi’s own floor** — starting with an engine manufacturing line in Kyoto — to gather operational data for auto-specialized AI robots
- **Build Highlanders machines** using Mitsubishi’s mass-production engineering, quality, durability, and mechatronics stack
- **Capital**: Mitsubishi has already invested in Highlanders and says it plans more

## Why Japan’s Autocomplete Matters

CEO **Takao Kato** told the Tokyo press conference that Japan is the “leadoff hitter” in mass-producing humanoids — a pointed contrast to U.S. and Chinese programs already in the news cycle. Highlanders CEO **Hiroya Masuoka** was blunter about the gap: he said Japan still lacks a company that can compete globally in “physical AI,” and the partnership is how they take a step.

Labor shortage is the domestic business case. Bipedal generalists are the bet that car-plant know-how transfers to robot BOM, line layout, and durability — the boring stuff that decides whether 1,000 units a month is a factory schedule or a slide deck.
