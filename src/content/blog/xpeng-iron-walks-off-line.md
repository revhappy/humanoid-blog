XPeng Inc. (NYSE: XPEV; HKEX: 9868) says its humanoid production lines in Guangzhou are now running, and a finished **IRON** walked off after automated assembly without a remote operator. The company dated the announcement **7 September 2026**. This is a commissioning event, not a claim that volume production has started.

## What the line actually is

XPeng calls the setup the first automated production lines for an “advanced general-purpose” humanoid, mixing automotive-grade quality systems with robot assembly. **More than 80%** of core processes are automated, according to the company press release and CnEVPost’s write-up of the same announcement.

CnEVPost is explicit about the gap: commissioning is not mass production. XPeng restated that mass production is still scheduled for **year-end 2026**, with first deployments in its own stores and campuses. Xinhua, covering the same launch, lists the first jobs as customer reception, vehicle introductions, visitor guidance, inspections, and small-item handling. Official launch and deliveries in China and overseas are planned for **2027**. Capacity and price were not disclosed.

Chairman and CEO **He Xiaopeng** said the lines were built from scratch with no precedent, and that “today’s step is small.” Electrek reports he clipped a staff badge onto IRON at the ceremony.

## What’s on the robot

The production IRON carries the same headline specs XPeng has used since the November 2025 unveil:

- **76** degrees of freedom across the body
- **21** DoF in each hand
- A fully enclosed flexible lattice structure XPeng describes as balancing appearance and safety
- **Three** in-house **Turing** AI chips, up to **2,250 TOPS**, so the Physical AI foundation model can run on the robot

The company says that stack lets IRON perform complex tasks without remote operation. That is still a company claim. Electrek notes XPeng has not shipped a robot to a paying customer.

The same release recaps the **24 August** robotics round: more than **US$900 million** at a post-money valuation over **US$6.3 billion**. CnEVPost identifies the unit as **Dogotix**, with IDG Capital leading and Alibaba and Tencent as strategic investors.

## A Human's Take

One robot walking off a line is the right kind of evidence. It is still one robot. I care about yield, takt time, and whether the first store units do reception and inspections without a handler in the wings. December is the date XPeng put on mass production. Hold them to it.
