**Figure AI** is back on the floor at **BMW Group Plant Spartanburg** — this time with **Figure 03** on a **logistics sequencing** job, not body-shop sheet metal. BMW’s **June 25, 2026** press release and Figure’s **June 30** “F.03 Arrives at BMW” post describe the new work in **Hall 52**, an assembly and logistics hall.

## From Figure 02 body shop to Figure 03 sequencing

BMW says the **Figure 02** pilot in 2025 supported production of more than **30,000 BMW X3** vehicles over about **ten months**, inserting sheet-metal parts for welding — high speed, high accuracy, physically demanding work.

**Ulrich Wieland**, BMW Manufacturing VP of Production Control and Logistics:

> “Plant Spartanburg is the birthplace of humanoid robotics in BMW Manufacturing’s operational day-to-day activities. Having already successfully completed a pilot with Figure 02 in our body shop, we are now looking forward to deploying Figure 03 for a sequencing use case in logistics.”

**Brett Adcock**, Figure founder and CEO, said the 11-month Figure 02 deployment proved humanoids can be a “valuable asset” in a flexible manufacturing workforce, and that Figure is now tackling assembly and logistics hall complexity.

## What sequencing means on this line

BMW’s description of the new use case:

1. Parts arrive unsorted in larger containers  
2. Figure 03 picks and sorts them into a **sequencing trolley**  
3. The trolley moves to a collection point  
4. An automated tugger train or Smart Transport Robot carries parts to the install location for **just-in-sequence** assembly  

Figure’s write-up stresses that sequencing is not a fixed pick-and-place loop: parts shift, rotate, and occlude, so the robot must perceive and correct on the fly while combining manipulation and locomotion — including **pulling a heavy cart** on caster wheels. Figure attributes that whole-body control to **Helix 02**, its pixels-to-actions VLA.

## Hardware upgrades BMW and Figure list for 03

Adcock, via BMW’s release, lists new Figure 03 features aimed at plant use:

- Soft components for safety  
- Wireless charging for higher availability  
- Speech-to-speech audio  
- Improved hands with tactile sensors and palm cameras  

BMW frames humanoids as a complement inside its broader **Physical AI** and **iFACTORY** push at Spartanburg — including virtual planning tools and **AIQX** quality inspection — not a full replacement for the human workforce. Hall 52 builds BMW X3 variants and is being prepared for the electrified **iX5**.

## A Human's Take

Sequencing is the unsexy job that actually breaks fixed automation — messy bins, shifting parts, carts that never park in the same pixel. If Figure 03 holds cycle time and error rates here for months, not a demo week, that matters more than another laundry fold clip. I’m watching for BMW to publish hours, intervention rates, and whether this stays Hall 52 or spreads to other plants.
