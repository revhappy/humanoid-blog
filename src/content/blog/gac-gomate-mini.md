GAC-incubated **Huilun Technology** brought its fourth-generation wheel-legged **GoMate Mini** to the 2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing. Gasgoo says the demo mixed dynamic inspection, a live perception feed, a data platform, a new **triple-folding** pose, and an on-site spray-waterproofing challenge.

The company is not pretending this is a lab first look. NetEase’s Songguo Finance report (August 20) says the WRC unit came off a real post, booth **C325**, while other Minis stayed on duty.

## What the fold is for

The triple-fold is the product hook. Auto-plant work is not one job. Inspection is standing. Parts runs, fixture moves, and station-to-station totes want a load-bearing shape. Huilun’s pitch is one body that switches from patrol posture to transport.

Gasgoo lists a variable wheel-leg structure with **three modes**, more than **8 hours** on a charge, and the ability to right itself after a fall. The same stack is supposed to do high-tempo sorting, handling, and assembly on a car line, then flip to campus reception, delivery, and emergency response.

## The fleet numbers

Both Gasgoo and the Songguo write-up use the same operations snapshot:

- Nearly **50** GoMate Mini units in **Guangzhou, Hong Kong, Xiamen, and Tianjin**
- Seven flagship projects; longest continuous run **12 months**
- About **63,000 km** of inspection travel
- About **189,000** anomalies handled
- Workload they equate to roughly **20.5** full-time people

Those are company-supplied counts, repeated by both outlets. They are the reason this is a product story, not a booth dance. Songguo also says Huilun recently closed more than **1 billion yuan** in financing from a group that includes CRRC Guochuang, CMB International, and listed auto-parts firm Tiancheng Zikong.

## A Human's Take

A robot that folds into a cart is the kind of dumb-smart hardware I like. The 12-month run and the 63,000 km are the part I will keep asking about. If those patrols are still happening after WRC packs up, Mini is a real machine. If the spray booth is the last time it gets wet, we learned nothing.
