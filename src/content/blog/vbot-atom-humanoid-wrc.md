**Vita Power**, the company behind the consumer quadruped **Datou**, put a full humanoid on the floor at the 2026 World Robot Conference in Beijing.

The machine is **ATOM**. According to 36Kr and a same-day Sina Finance write-up of the launch, it stands **160 cm**, with a **180 cm** arm span, **95 cm** legs, and **31** body degrees of freedom. The body is wrapped in fabric. A screen sits on the face. Ear lights show status. The soles are built to run quiet.

## Built for cabinets, not cages

Most of Hall C this week is 1.7–1.8 m metal frames with dexterous hands. ATOM is shorter on purpose. 36Kr reports the height, reach, and waist motion were reverse-engineered from cabinets, desktops, door handles, and the kind of indoor turn radius a person already lives with.

The company opened **personal, enterprise, and developer** versions for reservation at the show. Sina Finance, citing IPO Early Know, dates the launch to **August 19**.

## The quadruped that paid for the bet

This is not Vbot’s first consumer robot. SAG’s Robotics 360 numbers, quoted by both 36Kr and Sina, put global quadruped shipments at about **26,000** in the first half of 2026. Unitree still leads at about **13,600** units (**52%**), down from **75%** a year earlier. Vbot shipped about **1,600** units, roughly **6%**.

Datou’s first sale took **6,540** pre-orders and nearly **100 million yuan**. Mass production started in May. 36Kr records **1.26 million** large-model interactions, **23,700 km** of accompanying mileage, **131,500 hours** of companionship, and **86%** two-week retention. Four OTAs in three months added 31 functions and changed 66 more.

At the same show Vbot also brought **Datou EDU-W**, a wheel-foot EDU variant. 36Kr says it hits **2.5 m/s** and, under the company’s own test conditions, uses **50%** less energy than the standard EDU.

The software pitch is a stack it calls **Vbot Embodied Genome**: **Vbot-OmniDuplex**, **Vbot-WorldModel**, and **Vbot-EvoMorph**, aimed at duplex interaction, action evaluation, and moving skills across quadruped, wheel-foot, and humanoid bodies.

## A Human's Take

I like that they started from “will this fit under a cabinet” instead of “can it run a 100-meter.” Reservations at a trade show are not a shipping date, and 31 DoF in fabric is still a demo until someone keeps one at home for a month.

The Datou numbers are the interesting part. If even a slice of that consumer loop transfers to a biped, ATOM is a product story. If it doesn’t, it is another 160 cm statue with a face screen.
