**Ant Group** is not dabbling in humanoids — it is building a portfolio. **CNBC** reported that Ant led a **¥500 million ($73.58 million)** pre-Series A for home-robot startup **Zeroth**, bringing Zeroth’s total raised to **¥1 billion**. CNBC’s PitchBook analysis calls Zeroth Ant’s **12th** investment in the sector **since the beginning of 2025**.

## Zeroth: home robots, phased plan

Zeroth — known in China as **Suzhou JoyIn Intelligent Technology** — was founded in **late 2024**. Founder **Guo Renjie** told CNBC the company is taking a phased path: companionship robots for **elderly care and pet care** first, then **children’s education**.

Other details from the CNBC interview and report:

- Co-investors: **Monolith**, **Geely Capital**, **37 Interactive Entertainment**, and **Hua Capital**
- Robots currently use chips from **Horizon Robotics**
- Company claim: **orders for more than 30,000 units**
- Company claim: first-half operating revenue **up 600%** year over year
- Plan to start **North America and Europe** sales this fall after local compliance

Zeroth also said it wants to cooperate with Ant’s **AI- and robotics-friendly version of Alipay**.

## Ant’s wider robotics stack

CNBC’s tracked Ant investments span full robot companies (**Galaxea**, **Unitree**) and parts/software names (**Linkerbot**, **Hypershell**, **Genrobot AI**). That is a vertical-ish bet: platforms *and* the supply chain under them.

Ant also built an in-house robotics arm. In **late 2024** it established **RobbyAnt** (Ant Lingbo Technology), which later shipped its own robot. The **Robbyant** site presents the company as Ant Group’s embodied-intelligence unit — “one brain for all robots” — with platforms including **Robbyant R2** and related stacks.
