Faraday Future held its 919 robotics launch on **19 September 2026** and put nine new device configurations on sale the same day. The compact **FF Master Mini** starts at **$9,990**. The full-size **All-New Futurist** starts at **$89,900**.

The company, which still sells itself as an Embodied AI ecosystem shop rather than a car-only maker, streamed the event and posted a recap with a YouTube cut.

## What went on sale

The 919 press release lists nine configurations across two forms (humanoid and quadruped) and five models. All nine are described as available for sale and delivery through FF.com, Amazon, and RobotShop.

- **Master Mini** (~95 cm, ~19.5 kg): education, open development, and robot soccer. Compute is listed as **48 / 117 / 200 TOPS** across Mini, Pro, and Ultra. Prices: **$9,990**, **$12,990**, **$17,990**, each with a Skills Package the company values at $1,000–$2,000.
- **All-New Futurist**: **51** active degrees of freedom, **71** with a dexterous-hand upgrade. **1,152 Wh** dual-battery pack. Standard **$89,900** (Skills Package valued at $10,000). Ultra **$129,900** with NVIDIA Jetson Thor, **700 TOPS**, and a Skills Package valued at $15,000. The press release claims about **eight hours** under standard working conditions; FF’s Futurist product page lists **over six hours** with hot-swap for the Standard model.
- **FX Aegis Mega**: mid-size wheel-legged, **50 kg** payload, **25 cm** continuous stairs, **80 cm** single obstacle, **$74,990**.
- **FX Aegis Hyper**: large industrial quadruped from **$137,900**.
- **FX Aegis Classic Ultra-W**: lighter wheel-legged security/companion at **$12,990**.

## Solutions, not just SKUs

FF also launched four packaged **Industry Productivity Solutions**: K-12 education, university research, security, and inspection. Master Mini sits at the center of the K-12 stack. Futurist leads the research stack. Aegis variants cover indoor patrols versus parks, plants, and high-risk sites.

Founder and CEO **YT Jia** put the strategy in one line:

> "FF is not building a single robot form that attempts to do everything, we are building a robotics ecosystem in which a generalized and adaptable EAI Brain supports multiple robot forms."

The same release cites an average robotics product contribution margin of about **30%** from Q2 results, a “6+1” sales-and-rental network, and a RoboShare rental MOU with Hifivebot. Those are company figures, not audited unit economics in the text we fetched.
