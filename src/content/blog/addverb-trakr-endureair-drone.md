A YouTube clip circulating today shows **EndureAir’s Sabal-20** lowering **Addverb’s Trakr** quadruped onto a concrete yard. The on-screen title is “EndureAir’s Sabal-20 drone deploying Addverb’s Trakr robotic dog.” Indian Defence News dated **16 August 2026** says the two Noida firms are pairing Addverb’s ground robots with EndureAir’s UAVs.

Addverb’s product page lists Trakr as a quadruped for inspection and patrol in oil and gas, warehouses, and refineries. It is not a humanoid. It is a four-legged scout that is now being lifted into the shot.

## What Trakr is on paper

Addverb sells two variants on **addverb.ai/trakr**:

- **Trakr 5**: **5 kg** payload. Marketing copy says it climbs steps up to **170 mm**. Standing height **280 mm**, crouch **150 mm**, mass **18 kg** including battery.
- **Trakr 20**: **20 kg** carry. The same page calls it the heavy-duty unit.

The spec table on that page (shown next to Trakr 5) lists **1.5 m/s**, **30°** climb, **120 mm** maximum step, **1.5 hour** endurance, **24 V / 10 Ah** pack, **90-minute** charge, and an optional **IP67** rating. Compute is an **i7** plus a **Jetson Orin** module. Sensors include **3D LiDAR** and a wide-angle camera. Optional add-ons: thermal, night vision, acoustic inspection, a servo arm, and explosion-proof housing.

## Who is flying it

Indian Defence News places **Addverb** as a 2016 warehouse-automation company (more than **350** customers in **25** countries) and **EndureAir** as an **IIT-Kanpur** spinout from **2018**. It says EndureAir raised **₹13.5 crore** in seed money led by Jalaj Dani, with Addverb’s founders participating, and later **₹30 crore** in DST RDI funding for **SABAL-200**, described as a **200 kg / 200 km / 2.5 hour** heavy-lift platform.

EndureAir’s own site shows high-endurance airframes meant to move payloads over terrain gaps. The clip in circulation is labeled **Sabal-20**, a smaller machine than that SABAL-200 write-up.

## A Human's Take

Air-dropping a 18-kilogram dog is a fun logistics trick. I care whether Trakr can stand up after the sling lets go, keep LiDAR lock, and walk a patrol without a joystick. The product page is unusually specific on motors and IP rating. The YouTube yard shot is the receipt I wanted. Show me a night run on grating, then I’ll treat the airlift as more than a launch photo.
