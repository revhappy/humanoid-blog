On **August 7** in Changsha, **CASBOT Hands** (Zhongke Huisi Embodied Intelligence) put three new dexterous hands on a table: **L1**, **D1**, and **M1**. The same event previewed an **F Series** tendon-driven biomimetic hand. RoboticsTomorrow carried the launch on **August 12**. The new company was formed in **July 2026** by CASBOT (Zhongke Huiling), **Lens Technology**, and **Hunan Huaxia Investment Group**.

## Three hands, one job each

The launch pitch is that one hand cannot cover every cell. L1 is the cheap, light, high-cycle option. D1 is the higher-DoF generalist with tactile sensing. M1 is modular: a standard palm plus swap-in fingertips.

- **L1**: fewer degrees of freedom, lower arm-tip mass, fast open/close for repetitive work. Four L1-equipped robots already played live guitar, bass, keys, and drums as the **CASBOT BAND** at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference.
- **D1**: five-finger, independent multi-joint motion, coordinated fingers, tactile perception.
- **M1**: “standardized hand body plus functional fingertips” for task-specific tips.
- **F Series** (preview): tendon-driven, higher DoF, compliant motion closer to a human hand.

The company also showed data gloves and a data-collection toolchain. Founder **Zhengtao Zhang**, also a researcher at the Chinese Academy of Sciences Institute of Automation, said the spin-out exists so those pieces can be productized instead of living only inside full humanoid platforms.

## What a reseller already lists

AIFITLAB’s product page for the **CASBOT Handle-L1** lists a **Standard** and a **Pro (tactile)** edition at **$2,700**, left or right, with a backorder note. The spec table on that page:

- **Size / mass**: 194 × 84 × 52 mm, about **610 g**
- **DoF**: 11 joints, **6 active**
- **Grip**: >10 kg vertical static; >25 kg horizontal / lift
- **Cycle claim**: 1,000,000 no-failure cycles
- **Bus**: RS485 / CANFD / EtherCAT; ROS2 on Windows, Linux, macOS
- **Pro tactile**: 0.15–20 N fingertip range, 0.1 N resolution, ≤5 ms response

Those numbers come from a reseller listing, not the Changsha press packet. Treat them as the L1 SKU that is already being sold, not as verified factory test data.

GGII’s 2026 H1 China dexterous-hand report, cited in the launch coverage, puts 2025 China sales at about **19,200** units (+236.84% YoY) and projects **70,200** in 2026. The same report estimates **57** financing deals from 2023 through May 2026, about **RMB 12.8 billion**. CASBOT Hands is now based in Xiangjiang New Area, Hunan, and says it will help build a dedicated dexterous-manipulation training ground.
