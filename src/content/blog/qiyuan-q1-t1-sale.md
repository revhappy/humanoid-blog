Qiyuan Robotics put two consumer machines on sale in Shanghai on **20 September 2026**. The **Q1** humanoid and the **T1** transformable robot both start at **19,999 yuan**. Shipments are slated to go out in order from **1 October**.

The brand sits under listed materials company Swancor New Material. IT之家 and 36Kr both covered the “Me and My Personal Robot” event the same day.

## What you can actually order

IT之家 lists four SKUs:

- **Q1**: 19,999 yuan. About **88 cm** tall, **22** force-controlled degrees of freedom, foldable into a backpack. 36Kr adds about **15 kg**.
- **Q1 Explorer**: 26,999 yuan, with SDK, HDK, and hardware expansion ports.
- **T1**: 19,999 yuan. About **100 cm** and **16 kg**. Wheel-foot humanoid and quadruped on one body. IT之家 lists **2–3 hours** of runtime and a **3 kg** carry load.
- **T1 Pro**: 29,999 yuan. Adds an NVIDIA **Orin Nano**, dual-form 360° obstacle avoidance, low-battery return-to-dock, voice summon, and follow-and-carry.

IT之家 says first authorized shops cover seven cities: Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Hangzhou, Xiamen, Wuhan, and Xi’an.

## Joints, models, and a takt time

IT之家, citing Peng Zhihui (稚晖君) as Swancor chairman and Agibot co-founder and CTO, says Qiyuan’s in-house “egg joint” weighs **260 g**, is **47 mm** across, and claims **85 N·m/kg** peak torque density. The same report says Q1 and T1 are the first robots hooked into Tencent Cloud **WorkBuddy**.

36Kr lists three named models: **PrimeMotion** (compliant motion), **PrimeGo** (follow in both body forms), and **PrimeVista** (multimodal talk). Swancor CEO **Tian Hua** is quoted:

> "Someone has to take the first step."

36Kr also reports a claimed line rate of one robot every **2.5 minutes** and **500 million yuan** of planned 2026 R&D. Those are company figures from the event, not a third-party count of units shipped.

The company calls Q1 the “world’s first personal robot” and T1 the “world’s first transformable personal robot.” That is marketing language. What is on the page is a price, a ship date, and a fold-up 88 cm humanoid.
