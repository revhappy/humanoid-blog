The bottleneck for high-value factory work is not “can a robot move a box.” It is whether a machine can feel a screw seat, a cable go taut, or a part start to slip.

**Tacta Systems** last week launched **TactaBot**, bundling its **Tacta Hand**, **Skill Capture** glove system, and **Dexterous Intelligence** AI model into one stack aimed at skilled manufacturing, according to [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/tacta-systems-takes-aim-high-skilled-manufacturing-work-tactabot/) and the company’s [product site](https://www.tactasystems.com/).

## What is in the box

CEO and co-founder **Vikram Pavate** told The Robot Report that vision and brains have closed a lot of the gap on human workers, but hands and touch still lag for high-value work. Tacta’s pitch is four blocks: hand, touch, skill transfer, and intelligence.

From the Robot Report interview and Tacta’s site:

- **Tacta Hand**: human-scale gripper with **five fingers** and **15 degrees of freedom** (three per finger). Each finger can lift up to **25 N** at the fingertip (about **2.5 kg**). Three-finger configs are also offered.
- **Fluidic tendon actuation**: cables with fluid driving pistons so motors and heat sit off the fingers. Pavate claims thin fingers, high stability/precision versus motor-in-finger designs, and up to **30 million cycles** on the mechanical side.
- **Tacta Sensor**: proprietary, smaller-than-a-grain-of-sand tactile sensors with a claimed dynamic range from **250 Pa to 700,000 Pa**, temperature precision of **0.1 °C**, **1 mm** pitch, and per-sensor ADC. Not MEMS-based, per Pavate. Fingertips are designed as replaceable consumables.
- **Tacta Glove**: **256** of the same sensors per fingertip plus motion capture on the back; wireless stream to a local host. Workers wear it while doing the job so force, motion, video, and temperature become training data without teleop or custom coding.
- **Dexterous Intelligence**: off-the-shelf pretrained model fine-tuned with glove/mocap data, then customer-site fine-tuning on real operations.

## Timeline and market focus

The company is based at **3240 Hillview Ave, Palo Alto**, per its site. Founders come from electronics manufacturing, and near-term focus is manufacturing and electronics reshoring rather than nursing homes or kitchens, though Pavate sketched those as longer-horizon examples in the Robot Report piece.

**First TactaBot shipments** are expected in **early 2027**, according to that coverage.

On the site, Tacta cites a Stanford 2026 AI Index figure that of tasks scoring 90% in controlled simulation, only 12% succeed in the real world — their framing for why touch-and-skill data matters.
