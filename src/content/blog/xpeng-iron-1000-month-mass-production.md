**XPeng** is pushing **Iron**, its humanoid platform, toward a mass-production cadence. **CnEVPost** reported **July 15, 2026**, citing **The Wall Street Journal** and people familiar with the matter, that the EV maker aims to lift monthly capacity for Iron to **more than 1,000 units** by the **end of this year**, with a global product roll-out path in **2027**.

## Capacity, stores, and a Guangzhou base

According to the same CnEVPost summary of the WSJ reporting:

- **Store sales assistants** in China: planned for **Q1 2027**  
- **Overseas stores**: later in **2027**  
- Roadmap previously stated: overseas markets from **Q2 2027**, with a longer path toward ordinary households from **2028**

To back production, XPeng began construction in **Q1 2026** on a humanoid robot base in **Guangzhou** of about **110,000 square meters**, spanning R&D validation, trial production, and scaled manufacturing.

On a **March 2026** earnings call, chairman and CEO **He Xiaopeng** said Iron would enter mass production by **end of 2026** with a year-end monthly capacity target of **more than 1,000 units** — the same capacity number now echoed in the mid-July report.

## Specs XPeng has already put on the record

From CnEVPost’s earlier technical coverage, pulled into the July piece:

- Height **178 cm**, weight **70 kg**  
- All-solid-state batteries  
- **Three** XPeng **Turing** AI chips totaling **2,250 TOPS**  
- Second-generation **VLA** (Vision-Language-Action) model  

He took personal “CEO” responsibility for the robotics unit in **June 2026** and reorganized the robotics center into new departments to accelerate commercialization. Physical AI-related R&D spend is expected to reach **RMB 7 billion** (~**$1.03 billion**) this year, per the report.

Morgan Stanley analysts, as cited via WSJ in the same article, expect China humanoid shipments of **50,000** units in **2026** and **100,000** next year — industry context, not an XPeng guarantee.

## A Human's Take

A thousand units a month is a factory claim, not a keynote vibe. What I care about is whether those robots show up as **working store assistants** in 2027 with real uptime, or as showroom mannequins that need a teleoperator in the back room. XPeng’s auto supply chain is a real advantage on the body; the product still has to survive a full retail shift.
