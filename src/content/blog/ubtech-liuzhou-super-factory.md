Walker S humanoids are hanging in a high-bay rack in Liuzhou while wheeled **Cruzr** units feed the line. **UBTECH** held the commissioning ceremony for its Industrial Humanoid Robot Super Smart Factory on **12 September**, according to China News Service and [IT Home](https://www.ithome.com/1/001/635.htm).

The floor is **14,000 m²**, **13.8 m** high. Design takt: one industrial humanoid every **10 minutes**. Planned annual capacity: more than **10,000** Walker S and Cruzr units.

## Robots on the material loop

China News Service describes the closed loop: Cruzr series machines destack, palletize, load, and haul. Final assembly uses cobots, assistive manipulators, unmanned carts, and **360°** rotating fixtures. A digital “brain” schedules inbound and outbound flow.

[Humanoids Daily](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/inside-ubtech-s-10-000-unit-super-factory-how-robots-are-building-humanoids-in-liuzhou) names the wheeled pair as **Cruzr Y1** and **Cruzr S2**, working with **Wali** forklifts and **Chitu** haulers under UBTECH’s **Yanshee MOM** plus Siemens **Intelligence Center X**. UBTECH and Siemens built a 1:1 Plant Simulation twin before the floor existed, to check tote returns and AGV buffers against that 10-minute beat.

NetEase’s factory recap, drawing on company figures, adds the hardware chores:

- More than **2,000** screws, **50-plus** specs, per chassis
- More than **four hours** of end-of-line tests (static function, motion accuracy, payload endurance, haul aging)
- A **65 m²** automated warehouse holding **112** finished humanoids, with claimed storage-density gain above **50%**

Founder **Zhou Jian**, quoted by China News Service, said the plant will keep Walker and Cruzr on real Liuzhou stations so the company can collect industrial scene data. Liuzhou party secretary **Zhang Zhuang**, speaking at the ceremony, called the jump from first unit to thousand-unit output to a 10,000-unit plan a one-year-plus climb, per Hong Kong Commercial Daily.

Capacity is not the same as a filled order book. Humanoids Daily notes UBTECH’s H1 2026 full-size humanoid revenue jump and a cost target under **$20,000** by **2030**; those are company aims, not this weekend’s output. IT Home restates Walker S2 at **1.76 m** with autonomous battery swap — product context, not a new spec from the ceremony.

## A Human's Take

The picture I care about is Cruzr restocking the line that builds Walker, not the 10,000-unit slide. A 10-minute takt with 2,000 fasteners only matters if the four-hour burn-in stays honest when the rack is full. Liuzhou is a real factory. The next receipt is how many of those hanging Walkers leave on a truck.
